## Full description (≤4000 chars)

```
Swap the endless scrolling for a few calm minutes of real thinking.

MindGrid gives you six fresh logic puzzles every day. They're the same for everyone, they take a few minutes each, and they're designed to be solved by reasoning, not luck.

SIX ORIGINAL PUZZLES
• Blocks: slice the grid into rectangles so each one matches its number.
• Pipes: rotate tiles until the whole network flows from the source.
• Link: draw one path through every cell, passing the numbers in order.
• Crowns: place one crown in every row, column and colour region, with no two touching.
• Glow: flip the lights in plus-shapes until the board goes dark.
• Balance: even out the suns and moons, with never three in a row.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Today: a new daily set of six puzzles in Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert.
• Journey: an endless level path that gets harder as you go.
• Archive: replay any day from the past year, free.
• Streaks and stats: track your daily streak, best times and progress.

MADE FOR FOCUS
• No account or sign-in needed.
• Works offline once installed.
• Unlimited undo, a reset button and gentle hints when you're stuck.
• Your progress saves automatically.
• Light and dark themes, sound and haptics you can switch off.
• Hide the timer for a slow, logic-first session.

Every puzzle is generated fresh and checked for a valid solution. Crowns and Balance puzzles are built to have a single logical answer.

Train your focus, memory and reasoning a few minutes a day. Download MindGrid and turn downtime into brain time.
```
