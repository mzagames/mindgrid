Privacy Policy

MindGrid: Daily Logic Puzzles · by MZA Games · Last updated: November 6, 2026

Summary

MindGrid does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (solved puzzles, times, streaks, settings and in-progress games) is saved only in your device's local browser storage so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can erase it any time in Settings → Reset progress or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Information we collect

None. MindGrid does not request permissions to access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files or any other personal data.

Hosting

The game's files are delivered from GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the files and protect its service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. MindGrid itself receives none of this data.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the game puts a short text summary of your results (for example your solve times) on your clipboard or in your device's share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to paste or share it.

Children

MindGrid is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/mzagames/mindgrid/issues or use the contact email shown on the MindGrid Google Play listing.
